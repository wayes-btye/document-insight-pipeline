Camden Research weekly note, 18 Dec 2026

Year-end retrospective on the boutique commercial-strategy advisory segment:

Headline: 2026 was a structurally challenging year for the segment. Pricing pressure, intensifying competition, and rapid shifts in buyer expectations have left most firms in our coverage either flat or modestly down on revenue versus 2025 plan.

Outliers in either direction:
- Apex Strategy Group's growth trajectory is the upside outlier
- Two named firms in our coverage (Tannery Insights now-acquired, and one other) experienced material declines
- Most firms (including Meridian and equivalent peers) have absorbed the pressure with single-digit declines or flat results

The firms entering 2027 with the strongest competitive position share three traits:
- Visible differentiation beyond methodology (technology, vertical, partnerships)
- Engagement model flexibility (multiple structures offered)
- Active investment in client retention (named account teams, partner-level engagement)

The firms entering 2027 most exposed share three traits:
- Pure-play advisory with no proprietary capability
- Inflexible commercial model
- Concentration in client portfolio without active diversification strategy
