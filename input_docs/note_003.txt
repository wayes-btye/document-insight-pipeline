Meeting summary: Relay Systems partnership review (consolidated)
Date compiled: 22 Sep 2026
Compiled by: Jenna Rodriguez
Distribution: Aisha Brennan, Sarah Patel, leadership@meridian
Status: decision required by 18 Oct

Background

This summary consolidates three Relay Systems meetings (15 Jul, 12 Aug, 8 Sep) and presents a recommended next step ahead of the October leadership session.

Meeting 1, 15 Jul (initial scoping)
Attendees: Marcus Tanaka (Relay VP Partnerships), Sarah Wei (Relay BD), Jenna Rodriguez (Meridian)

Initial exploratory call. Relay is a pre-IPO ($340M last round) software firm whose commercial intelligence product overlaps about 30% with our advisory deliverables. Conversation was early-stage. Both sides agreed to follow up in August.

Meeting 2, 12 Aug (mutual deep-dive)
Attendees: Marcus Tanaka, Sarah Wei, Jenna Rodriguez, Tom Whitaker (Meridian)

Walked through respective customer bases and engagement models. Strong overlap in mid-market SaaS segment, where Meridian has been losing renewals to Apex over the past two quarters. Relay's customer base would represent a warm-introduction pipeline that we currently lack. Discussed pricing structure at high level.

Meeting 3, 8 Sep (proposal walk-through)
Attendees: Marcus Tanaka, Jenna Rodriguez

Tanaka presented Relay's proposed co-sell motion: Relay SDRs surface our advisory engagements as upsell paths for their software customers, Relay takes 15% of first-year ACV, joint pilots launch in Q1 2027.

Tanaka mentioned Relay is talking to two other advisory firms. Apex is rumoured to be in their conversations.

Assessment

Attractive aspects:
- Direct exposure to mid-market SaaS segment where we've been losing renewals to Apex
- Relay's product is genuinely strong, the bundle is coherent for buyers
- Signals market positioning ahead of competitive pressure

Concerns:
- 15% ACV revenue share is steep given existing SDR overhead. Math only works at high deal sizes.
- We are not exclusive in Relay's plans
- Public partnership ties us to Relay's roadmap, outside our control
- Risk of being used as a comparable against Apex if Relay is talking to both

Recommendation

Pursue, but narrower than Relay proposes:
1. Joint marketing only (case study and webinar series) for Q1 2027
2. No revenue-share commitment at this stage
3. Exclusivity carve-out: Relay agrees not to enter a deeper partnership with Apex within the SaaS commercial-strategy segment for 12 months

Decisions needed
- Commitment to pursue (Y/N): by 12 Oct
- Negotiation lead assignment: by 12 Oct
- Counter-proposal draft: by 18 Oct (Tanaka requested mid-October timeline conversation)
