Meeting summary: November partner meeting (concentration risk paper)
Date: 12 Nov 2026
Attendees: Aisha Brennan, Jenna Rodriguez, Sarah Patel, David Kowalski, Olivia Brand
Compiled by: Olivia Brand

Sole agenda item: David and Jenna's paper on revenue concentration and FY27 strategic stance.

The paper

Headline numbers (recap):
- Top 3 clients = 49% of Q3 booked revenue (vs 41% same quarter last year)
- Crestline alone = 20% of Q3
- 5 of top 6 clients are now in active pricing or scope renegotiation

Three scenarios presented:

Scenario 1: Defend
Continue investing primarily in deepening with the largest clients. Accept the concentration but pair it with stronger structural relationships (account teams, named senior sponsors). Lower acquisition cost; higher concentration risk.

Scenario 2: Diversify
Accelerate mid-market new business and tolerate lower margin in that segment for 18 months to broaden the base. Higher acquisition cost; reduced concentration; potentially exposed to mid-market churn risk that's already visible (note_026).

Scenario 3: Vertical specialisation
Accept current revenue mix but invest in a credible vertical (most plausible: healthcare or fintech) over 24 months. Long-term differentiation; near-term margin neutral.

Discussion

Aisha pushed back on the binary framing. Her view: scenarios 1 and 2 are not mutually exclusive. We can defend the largest accounts and accelerate mid-market simultaneously, but the question is what we deprioritise to create the bandwidth.

Jenna conceded the point but pushed back on whether we have the bandwidth to do anything meaningful in two areas at once.

Sarah raised the segmentation question: the mid-market pain (Apex pricing, integration gap) makes acceleration hard. Without the connector roadmap delivered (see integration_roadmap_internal.txt), mid-market acceleration is fighting against the wind.

David noted the concentration risk has been worsening for 4 consecutive quarters. The longer the decision is deferred, the more constrained the optionality.

Decision

Aisha asked David and Jenna to bring back a Phase 2 paper in January with:
- Specific scenarios for "defend + accelerate" with bandwidth assumptions
- Trigger conditions that would force a decision rather than a continued straddle
- Cost of doing neither well, expressed in revenue terms

Action items
- David and Jenna: Phase 2 paper, draft 15 Jan 2027
- Sarah: confirm Salesforce connector delivery date with engineering for Phase 2 paper assumptions, 5 Dec
