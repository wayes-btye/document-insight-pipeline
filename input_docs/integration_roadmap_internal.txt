Research snippet: integration roadmap proposal (internal)
Compiled by: Rohit Mehta (Engineering), with input from Sarah Patel
Date: 15 Oct 2026
For: leadership decision

Question
Should we commit to building Salesforce and HubSpot connectors in 2027, and if so, in what order?

Demand evidence

Salesforce connector has been raised in the past quarter by:
- Lattice (decisive in their renewal, see note_001, note_024, note_039)
- Helmsley (raised on every monthly call, see note_013, note_029, note_050)
- Concorde (raised at QBR, see note_023, note_044)
- Marigold (lost deal, integration gap was decisive, see note_056)
- Crestline (mentioned at recent call, see note_011)

HubSpot connector raised by:
- BridgeWave (raised at multiple touchpoints, see note_014, note_036, note_055)
- Two prospects in Q3 pipeline (Pinion, Vellum) where the gap contributed to losses

Salesforce volume of demand is roughly 2x HubSpot in our current portfolio.

Engineering capacity

See note_022 for the full picture. Headline: with current team and assuming we successfully retain the at-risk seniors, we can deliver one connector in 2027 alongside committed platform work. We cannot deliver both.

If we lose either at-risk senior, we cannot deliver any connector work in 2027.

Recommendation

1. Commit to Salesforce connector for 2027 H1 delivery. Communicate this to Lattice, Helmsley, Concorde immediately as a positive signal. Use it to reframe Lattice renewal conversation specifically.

2. Defer HubSpot connector explicitly to 2027 H2 or 2028. Be honest with BridgeWave that this means no movement during their 2027 renewal scope.

3. Make the engineering retention question (note_022) the highest internal priority. Without retention this entire roadmap is unsupported.

4. Continue to track competitor connector positioning. If Apex demonstrates a HubSpot connector publicly, the cost of not having one rises sharply.

Open questions for partners
- Is there appetite for a small dedicated engineering hire to widen the connector capacity?
- Are we comfortable commiting publicly to the Salesforce roadmap before the engineering retention is stabilised?
