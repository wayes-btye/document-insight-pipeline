Camden Research, Apex Strategy Group year-in-review
12 Dec 2026

Apex's 2026 in summary:

Headcount nearly doubled (40 to ~95). Series B closed September. London headquarters retained, Boston office opened in Q1, Frankfurt office expected Q1 2027.

Pricing posture: aggressive entry pricing (30-40% below boutique competitors). Whether this is sustainable as Apex scales is the open question.

Vertical signalling: ex-Bain healthcare hire suggests deliberate vertical positioning. Public thought leadership in healthcare visible from October.

Wins (publicly known): three confirmed competitive wins against named boutique competitors. Multiple suspected wins in mid-market segment.

Losses (publicly known): one named loss to a specialist firm in pricing analytics. Otherwise quiet on losses.

Outlook for 2027

Apex's 2027 priorities, based on hiring patterns and public statements: consolidate the mid-market wins from 2026, build out EU presence (Frankfurt office), develop healthcare vertical credibility through case studies and thought leadership, continue aggressive sales motion.

The open question: can Apex sustain its pricing differential as it scales delivery? Buyer reports of delivery slippage have begun to surface (one in our coverage). If quality concerns emerge as a sustained pattern, the price-led entry strategy becomes harder to maintain.
