Research snippet: mid-market segment health, Q3 review
Compiled by: Ben Markovich
Date: 26 Sep 2026
For: Sarah Patel, Jenna Rodriguez

Definition: mid-market = clients with 50-500 employees, ARR $80k-$300k for us.

Q3 segment composition
- 11 active mid-market clients
- Q3 booked revenue from segment: $1.6M (34% of total)
- Average ARR: $145k
- 2 mid-market renewals closed Q3 (BridgeWave bridge extension at reduced scope, Elemental Labs at flat)
- 3 renewals deferred from Q3 to Q4 (unusual)

Engagement health signals across the 11 accounts
- Green: 4 accounts (clear renewal intent, expansion conversations active)
- Amber: 5 accounts (renewal expected but on tighter scope, pricing pressure visible)
- Red: 2 accounts (Northpoint, plus one more added this quarter: Stellaris)

Stellaris worth flagging
The Stellaris account has gone quiet over the past 6 weeks. Account manager has not been able to schedule a check-in. Renewal in March. Suggest treating as red until proven otherwise.

Cross-cutting observations

Three patterns repeat across the amber/red accounts:
1. Stated budget pressure, often citing CFO-led vendor reviews
2. Scope-reduction asks rather than direct discount asks (perhaps to avoid signalling churn intent)
3. Apex named or implied in 6 of the 7 amber/red accounts as alternative under consideration

The mid-market segment is where competitive pressure from Apex is most directly visible. The integration gap (Salesforce, HubSpot) is a recurring secondary concern in this segment.

Recommendation

Treat mid-market as a strategic risk segment for FY27 planning. Specifically:
- Conduct red-flag retention conversations with the 2 red accounts within 30 days
- Bring forward the connector roadmap discussion to October partner meeting
- Reconsider whether mid-market segment requires its own engagement model rather than a scaled-down enterprise approach
