Camden Research, advisory pricing trends note
21 Sep 2026

Boutique commercial advisory pricing - Q3 review

Median day-rates across our covered firms have declined 4-6% versus same quarter last year. Three primary drivers identified:

1. Procurement tightening at the buy side. Several large clients now require comparable benchmarking on professional services renewals. The benchmarking exercise itself drives modest pricing concessions.

2. Apex Strategy Group's market entry pricing (covered separately). The 30-40% discount to incumbent pricing has reset some buyer expectations even where buyers do not switch.

3. Macro budget pressure. Multiple firms cite client conversations referencing CFO-led vendor consolidation reviews. This appears to be a sector-wide phenomenon rather than firm-specific.

Outlook into 2027

We expect continued downward pressure on day-rate pricing, with stronger differentiation in deal structure (milestone-based vs retainer) and bundled scope offerings (advisory + technology).

Firms with proprietary tooling or technology assets are better positioned to defend pricing through differentiation. Pure-play advisory firms face the most direct exposure.
