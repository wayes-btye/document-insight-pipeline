Research snippet: Apex Strategy Group competitive brief
Compiled by: Ben Markovich
Date: 21 Sep 2026
Purpose: standing reference for engagement leads

Background

Apex Strategy Group founded 2019. Headquartered London, second office Boston (opened Q1 2026). Recent Series B closed September 2026 at $180M valuation, $42M raised (Tarn Capital lead). Headcount grown from ~40 to ~95 over past 18 months.

Positioning

Apex positions as "data-led commercial advisory for mid-market." Their narrative emphasises proprietary benchmarking data assembled across their client engagements. We have not been able to verify the depth of this data asset.

Pricing

Public pricing is not disclosed but we have direct knowledge from three buyer conversations:
- Lattice Logistics (Sep): Apex quoted $52k for scope Lattice characterised as comparable to our $78k engagement
- BridgeWave (Sep): Apex "approached with a different framing", pricing not shared with us
- Forge Analytics (Sep): Apex shortlisted alongside one other firm for a pricing project we were not invited to

The pattern is consistently 30-40% below our list pricing. Whether the scope is genuinely comparable is the open question.

Where we have lost in 2026
- Three confirmed losses (one Q1, two Q2)
- Two suspected losses (Q3, where the buyer disengaged after Apex contact)

Where Apex is weak
- Limited senior bench: their named partners are well-credentialled but the delivery teams are junior-heavy
- No verticalisation: they pitch the same offering across SaaS, services, and (recently) healthcare
- Reference-thin in segments where they're newest

Recommended posture

Continue selling on depth and senior involvement. Avoid getting drawn into scope-by-scope pricing comparisons in early-stage conversations. When pricing pressure surfaces, frame around outcome not input.

Worth tracking
- Apex hiring announcements (LinkedIn shows 8 senior hires since Series B announcement)
- Any sign of Apex partnership announcements (Relay rumour worth watching, see note_003)
