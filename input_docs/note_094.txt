Camden Research, year-end commercial advisory review (extract)
Issued 17 Dec 2026

The boutique commercial advisory segment in 2026 has been characterised by three structural shifts:

1. Pricing compression. Median day-rates declined 4-6% across our coverage universe. Procurement-driven renewal benchmarking is now the default at large clients.

2. Capability differentiation has shifted from methodology to product. Firms with proprietary tooling or technology integration capability are defending pricing more effectively than pure-play advisory firms.

3. Mid-market segment competitive pressure has intensified. Apex Strategy Group's emergence as an aggressive mid-market bidder has been the single largest competitive dynamic this year. Multiple firms in our coverage have lost mid-market accounts to Apex on price-led pitches.

Outlook for 2027

We expect continued pressure on pricing, with successful firms differentiating through engagement-model flexibility, technology integration, and credible vertical specialisation. Firms that cannot articulate a clear differentiation story by mid-2027 will likely face material renewal exposure.

Notable: regulatory complexity (EU AI Act, similar regimes in other jurisdictions) is emerging as a meaningful service-line opportunity for firms with the relevant credibility.
