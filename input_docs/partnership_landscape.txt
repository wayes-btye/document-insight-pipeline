Research snippet: SaaS commercial-strategy partnership landscape
Compiled by: Sarah Patel
Date: 4 Oct 2026
For: Aisha Brennan, Jenna Rodriguez

Question being addressed
Should the Relay Systems partnership decision be considered in isolation, or as one of several partnership opportunities we should evaluate as a coherent strategy?

Current partnership-relevant opportunities

1. Relay Systems (active). See note_003 and note_037. Decision needed mid-October. Co-sell motion proposed by Relay; we are recommending narrower joint marketing arrangement.

2. Camden Research. Informal discussion at sector conference last month. Camden interested in distributing our analyst commentary to their subscriber base. Low cost, modest reach. No revenue share required. Decision-maker on Camden side: Sarah Olafsson.

3. PitchPoint (early stage). Sales engagement platform with mid-market SaaS focus. Initial conversation on 2 Oct. They are actively seeking advisory partners to broaden their value proposition. Earlier-stage than Relay; more flexibility on terms but also more risk.

4. Verdant Therapeutics referral pattern. See note_005, note_028. Karen Doerr has referred two prospects in 60 days. This is a referral relationship not a formal partnership but worth recognising.

Comparison

Relay is the highest-impact opportunity but also the highest commitment cost. Camden is essentially free distribution. PitchPoint is the most uncertain. The Verdant pattern is unstructured but real.

Strategic question

If we treat Relay as the primary partnership and the others as supplementary, the Relay terms get the most negotiation attention.

If we treat partnership formation as a strategic capability we want to build (a partnership function), then we should consider whether to invest in someone with partnership-development experience. We don't currently have that capability internally.

Recommendation

Make the Relay decision on its own merits in October but begin a structured discussion in November about partnership strategy as a capability question. The pattern across these four opportunities suggests we will face this kind of decision repeatedly over the next 18 months.
