Camden Research, sector signal note 23 Sep

Confirmation that Relay Systems has formally entered partnership selection process with at least four boutique advisory firms in the SaaS commercial-strategy segment. Selection expected end-October. Relay's stated criteria include "delivery quality on existing client engagements" and "willingness to commit to revenue-share economics."

Two of the four firms have published joint marketing content with Relay in the past 30 days. The other two (one of which is Meridian) have not.

The market read is that Relay is using the partnership selection process as a competitive marketing exercise as much as a genuine commercial decision. Firms that move slowly may find themselves observing rather than participating.
