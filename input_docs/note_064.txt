Research snippet: Apex / Relay rumour - update
Compiled by: Ben Markovich
Date: 18 Oct 2026

Following the Relay partnership decision (see note_063), reviewing whether Apex's involvement is fact or rumour.

Sources reviewed
- Camden's published sector notes (Aug-Oct)
- LinkedIn activity of Apex senior team
- Direct buyer conversations

Findings

The "Apex courting Relay" story was first surfaced in note_003 as a rumour from Marcus Tanaka's August conversation. It has not been substantiated since.

LinkedIn analysis: no joint content between Apex and Relay senior staff. By contrast, two of the other partnership-shortlisted advisory firms have published joint LinkedIn content with Relay in the past 30 days.

Buyer conversations: of the four Relay-customer-base buyers we've spoken with in the past quarter, none have mentioned an Apex-Relay relationship. They have mentioned Apex generally.

Conclusion

The Apex / Relay angle was likely a negotiation tactic by Tanaka rather than a substantive parallel conversation. Relay is using competitive tension as leverage in selecting a partner.

Implication: our exclusivity carve-out request may be rejected if Apex is not actually a real alternative for Relay. Worth Aisha being aware before the next Tanaka conversation.
