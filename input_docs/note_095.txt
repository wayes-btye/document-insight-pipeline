Sector outlook 2027 - Camden Research forecast extract
2 Dec 2026

Five themes Camden expects to dominate the boutique commercial advisory landscape in 2027:

1. Acceleration of integration capability development. The firms that ship CRM connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics) in H1 2027 will reset the differentiation baseline.

2. Continued M&A consolidation. Mid-size firms are likely acquisition targets. The number of independent boutique advisory firms in our coverage will likely contract by 15-20% by end of 2027.

3. Vertical specialisation as a defensive moat. Firms that establish credible vertical practices (healthcare, financial services, regulated industries) are better positioned to defend pricing.

4. Partnership formalisation between advisory firms and software vendors. Multiple announcements expected through 2027 as both sides seek to expand value propositions.

5. EU AI Act compliance work emerging as a meaningful service line. Estimated market size for the SaaS-buying-customer segment alone: $200-400M by 2028.

Underlying drivers

Buyer-side procurement sophistication continues to increase. Buyers are more willing to switch advisors than they have been historically. The combination of price-led entrants (Apex) and the integration-capability arms race creates structural incumbency risk for firms that have not articulated a clear differentiation story.
