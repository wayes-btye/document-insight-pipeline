Meeting summary: Verdant Therapeutics discovery call
Date: 4 Oct 2026
Attendees: Marcus Chen, Sarah Patel (Meridian), Anita Sandhu (Verdant CEO), Robert Kane (Verdant Head of Commercial)

First call with Verdant, introduced by Karen Doerr (Lattice). 45 min.

Verdant context

Series C biotech, ~280 employees, lead asset entering Phase III. Anita expects commercial launch readiness work to begin Q1 2027 ahead of a 2028 launch.

What they're looking for

Commercial strategy advisory specifically focused on:
- Pricing and reimbursement strategy for the lead asset
- Channel design (direct sales force vs partnerships vs distributor models for different geographies)
- Launch sequencing across markets

Anita was direct that they have spoken to two specialist healthcare firms. Both engagements would have been comfortable for her but came in at "twice the budget envelope I have available." She's looking for whether a generalist commercial firm with biotech support could deliver something useful at our price point.

Robert pressed us on healthcare experience. Sarah was honest: we don't currently have direct biotech client experience. She walked through our adjacent commercial work and offered the perspective that pricing strategy frameworks transfer across sectors with the right context layer.

Robert's response: "I've heard versions of that pitch before and I'd want to see how you'd actually do it before I'd commit."

Ask: would we be willing to scope a small paid pilot (4-6 weeks, $30-40k) on one specific question, pricing benchmarking for their therapeutic class. If the pilot lands well, broader engagement follows.

Anita and Robert will discuss internally and come back next week.

Read

This is exactly the kind of opportunity Helen Ashworth flagged in her interview (see interview_healthcare_expert_20260818). A discounted/pilot engagement that builds case-study credibility. Worth taking seriously even if margin is thin.
