Notable transactions in commercial intelligence and adjacent advisory:

- A specialist healthcare consultancy (mid-size, ~$25M revenue) acquired by an enterprise consulting group. Deal value undisclosed but estimated 8-10x revenue. Suggests strong M&A interest in healthcare-specialist firms.

Continued sector consolidation suggests the boutique-firm landscape will look meaningfully different by mid-2027.
