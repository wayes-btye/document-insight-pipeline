Camden Regulatory Brief, EU AI Act enforcement readiness update
8 Dec 2026

Status update on EU AI Act Phase 2 enforcement (effective 2 Aug 2027):

- Five member states have published draft inspection frameworks (Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands). Remaining member states expected to follow Q1 2027.
- The Spanish framework's broader interpretation of "operator" applicability (covered in note_054) has been adopted in modified form by Italy. Pattern suggests advisory firms producing AI-derived analysis should plan for this scope rather than the narrower one.
- A working group on cross-border enforcement coordination has been formed under the European AI Office. First report expected Q2 2027.

For advisory firms with EU exposure: readiness work that is not started by mid-Q1 2027 will be increasingly difficult to complete before the August deadline. Specialist capacity is constrained and pricing is firming.

Recommended Q1 actions for in-scope firms:
- Conduct internal scope assessment
- Engage specialist counsel for ambiguous obligations
- Document model usage in client engagements going forward
- Begin client communication on the firm's compliance position
