Camden Research, sector talent moves note (extract)
2 Oct 2026

Notable senior moves in the SaaS commercial advisory space, Q3:

- Two senior consultants from Tannery Insights moved to Apex following Apex's Series B announcement
- A former Bain principal joined Apex as Head of Healthcare (this signals Apex's stated healthcare expansion is gaining real bench)
- One named partner at Halberd Tech departed for a corporate strategy role

Engineering and product hiring across the segment has been strong since June. Compensation benchmarks have moved up an estimated 7-10% for senior backend roles in the past two quarters. Firms not actively reviewing comp are likely losing competitiveness on retention.
