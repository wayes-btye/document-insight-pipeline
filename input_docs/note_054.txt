Camden Regulatory Brief: AI Act enforcement coordination
27 Sep 2026

Brief update for advisory firms tracking the EU AI Act phase 2 timeline.

Member states have begun appointing enforcement coordination leads within their national competent authorities. Germany and France have published their draft inspection frameworks. Spain expected to follow in October.

Of practical interest: the Spanish framework explicitly contemplates that B2B advisory firms producing AI-derived analysis may be subject to operator-level documentation obligations even where they are not the deployer of the AI system. This is a stricter interpretation than initially expected.

Recommended action for in-scope firms: do not delay readiness work in expectation that scope interpretations will narrow. They are narrowing in some directions and broadening in others.
